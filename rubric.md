# EEET2588 Project Implementation Rubric

## 1. Project Demonstration

**Maximum: 50 points**

### Assessment criteria

- Clear, well-organised, and professional demonstration of the implemented solution and its key features and scenarios.
- Demonstrates sound understanding of the system, including its operation, design decisions, implementation, and testing.
- Effectively responds to Q&A, including explaining, justifying, and re-demonstrating aspects of the solution when requested by the assessor.
- Individual performance and contribution, including demonstrated understanding, participation, and ability to explain implementation details.

### Rating scale

| Rating | Mark Range | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| **HD** | 39.5–50 | Outstanding and unequivocally excellent demonstration, providing comprehensive and convincing evidence of achievement. Demonstrates exceptional technical understanding and responds confidently, accurately, and effectively during Q&A. Individual performance and contribution are clearly demonstrated at an exceptional level. |
| **DI** | 34.5–<39.5 | Very good demonstration showing a high level of achievement and technical understanding, with only minor flaws or limitations. Responds effectively to Q&A, with strong evidence of individual performance and contribution. |
| **CR** | 29.5–<34.5 | Good demonstration showing sound achievement and understanding, but with some noticeable flaws, limitations, or inconsistencies in the demonstration, explanations, or Q&A. Individual performance and contribution are clearly evident. |
| **PA** | 24.5–<29.5 | Acceptable demonstration of the essential project outcomes, but with noticeable weaknesses in demonstration, explanation, Q&A, or individual performance and contribution. |
| **NN** | 0–<24.5 | Insufficient demonstration with significant missing evidence, technical issues, limited understanding, inadequate responses during Q&A, and/or insufficient evidence of individual performance and contribution. |

## 2. Overall Technical Outcome

**Maximum: 50 points**

### Assessment criteria

- Overall correctness, completeness, and quality of the implemented solution against the project requirements.
- Appropriate design and integration of system components, real-time behaviour, communication, synchronisation, and timing mechanisms.
- Reliability, robustness, testing, and handling of relevant operating conditions or failures.
- Quality of the software implementation, including structure, maintainability, and appropriate use of QNX/POSIX mechanisms.
- Technical justification, sophistication, and ingenuity where appropriate.
- Individual performance and contribution.
